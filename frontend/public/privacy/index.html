Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026. The short version: there are no accounts and no tracking cookies, and your voice is never stored.

What is stored

MissionsThe task details you typed into the form, the agents' log and trace (what each agent read, wrote and how long it took, shown in the inspector), and the finished deliverable, so the result can be shown, downloaded and (if you choose) shared. Deleted automatically after 30 days, and sooner on request.
CallsOnly when the call started and ended, its length, how much AI capacity it used, and how it ended. No audio and no transcript is stored.
Text chatMessages are processed to produce a reply and are not stored by this site.
Questions in the menuA plain-language question typed into the Ctrl/⌘ + K menu ("show me his backend work") is sent to the server, and to the AI provider when live AI is on, only to pick where to take you. It is not stored.
Live presenceWhile the site is open, your pointer position (as a fraction of the window) and the section you're viewing are passed on to other visitors as an anonymous dot, under a random id that changes on every visit. Nothing about it is stored or logged, and no name or IP address is ever shared.
IP addressKept with missions and calls, only to apply the daily limits (a few runs per visitor per day) and to stop abuse. Deleted with them after 30 days.
In your browserA "sound on/off" and a "graphics full/lite" preference (local storage) and a flag that the intro has played (session storage). No cookies are set by this site.

Who else sees it

  • AI providers. When live AI is enabled, what you type (or the text of what you say) is sent to a language-model provider (Groq and/or Anthropic) to write the reply. For voice calls, your speech is sent to Whisper (via Groq) to be turned into text, and the AI's reply text is sent to Fish Audio to be spoken. This site does not keep the audio; the providers handle it under their own policies.
  • Bot check. Where enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person before a call or mission starts.
  • Hosting. The site and its API run on a hosting provider and a database provider, which see requests as any host does.
  • Analytics. If enabled, a privacy-friendly, cookie-less counter (Plausible) records page views and whether the Call and Mission features are used, with no personal profile.
  • Shared plans. If you press Share on a deliverable, anyone with that link can read it, including the details you typed into the task. Don't share it if it contains anything private. The link stops working when the plan is deleted after 30 days.

Please don't

Don't enter private, confidential or customer data into a mission or call. The agents are a demo: they work from a template and the portfolio's own content, not from your files.

Your choices
